## 📌 Deliverables Overview

### 1. Google Colab Notebook
* **Link:** `https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1UTJ0NDZTffQQ-if6o_TP_OBLeP15rkYi?usp=sharing`
* **Features:** 20 runnable cells covering 1,200 students across 3 institutes (Patna, Kota, Lucknow), 24 milestone tests, 2PL Item Response Theory (IRT) parameter recovery ($r = 0.978$ & $r = 0.989$), Bayesian Knowledge Tracing topic mastery heatmaps, Leave-One-Institute-Out early warning dropout classification, Split-Conformal prediction intervals $[P_{10}, P_{90}]$ with $91.5\%$ empirical coverage, and deterministic bilingual English/Hindi parent digests with factual guardrails.

### 2. Excel Financial Model
* **Link:** `https://1drv.ms/x/c/d60414ee7b60239b/IQDt8Tm9OCY0TrB-BuRbFUM4AdbpgmjsYKaEo6IS4nLO-gE?e=u1YbJe`
* **Features:** 5 fully linked sheets:
  1. `Assumptions & Drivers`: 400 students/institute, ₹60,000 annual fee, 12% baseline dropout rate, ₹36k uncollected fee loss per dropout.
  2. `Institute ROI Calculator`: 8 students retained per institute $\rightarrow$ ₹2,88,000 tuition fee recovered $\rightarrow$ **2.22x ROI on Nextute Tier 2** (4.5-month payback).
  3. `Platform Unit Economics`: ₹12,999/mo Tier 2 pricing, 72.4% gross margin, 16.5x LTV/CAC ratio.
  4. `3-Year Projections`: Growth from 15 to 320 institutes, ₹4.82 Cr ARR by Year 3.
  5. `Sensitivity Analysis`: 2-way matrix stress-testing retention rate vs pricing.
